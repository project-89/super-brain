# Provenance

`@_89/fold-trajectory` is repository-owned delivery code. It composes the local
`@_89/fold-trace` projection and divergence contracts with the local
`@_89/fold-eval` review protocol, then persists their inputs as canonical Fold
records. No source was copied from an external repository.

The projection and evaluation mechanics retain their own pinned evidence and
provenance records. This package adds lifecycle, replay, scope, and API-facing
contracts only; it does not expand the parity claims of either dependency.
